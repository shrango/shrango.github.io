Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · Washington University in St. Louis

I am a third-year PhD student at Washington University in St. Louis (WUSTL), luckily advised by Prof. Jiaxin Huang.

Before that, I received my Master degree at Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT/CAS) in Jul. 2024, where I studied at ICT Natural Language Processing (ICTNLP) group and worked on machine translation and multilingual NLP.

CV Google Scholar DBLP GitHub h.langlin [at] wustl.edu

Research

My research interests mainly lie in Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models. Particularly, I am interested in:

  • 01Agentic RL and On-Policy Distillation
  • 02Large Language Model Reasoning & Post-Training
  • 03Large Language Model Efficiency
